# Koode Terms of Use

*Effective: August 2026*

1. **What Koode is.** Koode is a personal journey companion that shares your
   live journey status with people you explicitly approve. It is provided
   free of charge, as-is, without warranty of any kind.

2. **What Koode is not.** Koode is **not an emergency service** and not a
   substitute for one. The SOS feature alerts your approved viewers — it does
   not contact police, ambulance or any authority. In an emergency, call your
   local emergency number. Koode's wellbeing displays are factual logs, not
   medical advice or monitoring.

3. **Accuracy depends on conditions.** Location, ETA and Journey Health
   depend on GPS quality, network coverage and phone battery. Updates can be
   delayed or unavailable; the app labels stale data honestly, but you should
   never rely on Koode as the sole safeguard for anyone's safety.

4. **Your responsibilities.** Share Journey IDs only with people you trust;
   approve viewers thoughtfully; don't use Koode to track anyone without
   their knowledge and consent — it is built to make that hard, and doing so
   may be illegal where you live. Do not interact with the app while driving.

5. **Data.** Handled as described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) —
   in short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, journey data self-destructs
   one hour after you end the journey, and everything else stays on your phone.

6. **Open source.** Koode's source code is public. Contributions are welcome;
   the software is distributed in the hope that it is useful, without
   liability for how it is used.
